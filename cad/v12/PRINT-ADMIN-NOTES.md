# Notes for the operator

Print v12 only. The delivery ZIP contains ten STLs: print one of each. No coupons or gauges. Use PETG, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid and 6 top/bottom layers. Set the two C clamps to 100% infill. Millimeters, 100% scale. Preserve the supplied orientations: case back-down/open side up, cover exterior-down, clamps and camera carrier on their broad sides, camera guard OPEN RIM DOWN with lens opening and four bumpers UP, shoes and knobs with their blind/hex pockets up. The guard has a 45-degree interior and a 6 mm bridge above its small service notch; inspect these and the two thin snap tongues in the layer preview. Use a brim on the guard, including its small clip-tip footprints. Use selective removable supports only where the layer preview requires them; keep supports out of screw pilots, nut pockets and clip gaps. Check the remaining small bridges, horizontal holes and logo recess. Use the calibrated PETG profile and normal speed. No heat-set inserts. Parts 01, 03 and 04 changed from v11; use the v12 guard and carrier together. The STLs have not been sliced into a printer-specific project or G-code.

# Notes for the operator

Print the v11 installed parts only; no coupons or gauges. The delivery ZIP has ten STLs: print one of each. In the source STL folder, print two each of 05 clamp, 06 pressure shoe and 07 hand knob, and one each of 01–04. Use PETG, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid and 6 top/bottom layers. Override the two C clamps to 100% infill. Millimeters, 100% scale. Preserve the supplied orientations: case back-down/open side up, cover exterior-down, clamps and camera carrier on their broad sides, guard on its supplied flat rear side, shoes and knobs with their blind/hex pockets facing up. See the two plate-layout images. Use selective removable supports only where the sliced preview needs them; the large port lintels are removed and the remaining small bridges, clips, screw holes and logo recess need review. Keep supports out of pilot holes, captive-nut pockets and clip gaps. Add a brim only where needed for adhesion. Use the calibrated PETG profile and normal speed. No heat-set inserts are used. These STLs have not been sliced into a printer-specific project or G-code.

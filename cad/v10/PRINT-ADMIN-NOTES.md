# v10 print operator notes

Copy the following paragraph into **Notes for the operator**:

Please use the v10 STLs, PETG, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 6 walls, 30% gyroid infill, and 6 top/bottom layers, at 100% scale in millimeters. Print the frame gauge and screw-fit coupon first; wait for their fit checks before printing the large body. Keep the supplied orientations: main body on its side (68.65 × 180.5 × 214 mm), cover outer-face-down, camera guard on its side, and screw coupon on its side (44 × 10 × 52 mm) with horizontal holes. Use 100% infill modifiers around both retaining arms, including roots and toes. Use removable supports and a brim for the main body; inspect support access at the distant arm, camera cradle, port openings and bosses. Add local supports to the cover/guard/coupon only as needed. Do not put supports inside the small pilot bores or clip gaps. Preserve the coupon's 0.5 mm label strokes and inspect the sliced hole paths. Main-body pilot diameters are 1.7 mm for M2 and 2.1 mm for M2.5; if another coupon size fits better, request revised CAD rather than scaling the part. No heat-set inserts are required. This is an untested functional prototype; support placement and slice review are still required.

See the [full print instructions](../../docs/hardware/v10/printing.md) and [assembly guide](../../docs/hardware/v10/assembly.md).
